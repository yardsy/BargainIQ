# BargainIQ launch gates

Private beta remains noindex until all public launch gates are verified.

- [ ] V11 account browser tests and database ownership checks pass.
- [ ] V12 importer, matching, analytics and commercial validation tests pass.
- [ ] Genuine inventory from approved feeds is fresh and verified.
- [ ] Check production mobile navigation, accessibility and error states.
- [ ] Confirm privacy notice covers active features and data retention.
- [ ] Confirm account removal and notification unsubscribe.
- [ ] Verify email sender, explicit notification opt-in and duplicate prevention.
- [ ] Activate analytics only after abuse controls and admin-only aggregate reporting are tested.
- [ ] Verify affiliate network approval and exact authorised destinations.
- [ ] Disclose commissions beside relevant links; keep organic ranking independent.
- [ ] Sponsored offers must be labelled and approved; no paid placement without agreement.
- [ ] Confirm final domain and brand; add canonical URLs, accurate category metadata and sitemap.
- [ ] Remove noindex from public pages only after approval; keep private/account pages unindexed.
- [ ] Verify robots headers and sitemap against production URLs.
- [ ] Obtain explicit approval before any new paid subscription or advertising spend.

Do not use synthetic listings as real inventory, submit private beta pages for indexing, or treat test analytics as customer traffic.
