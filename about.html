About BargainIQ

BargainIQ is an Australian shopping comparison development pilot. It is not a retailer and does not process purchases.

Prices and listings

Prices are in Australian dollars. Verified offers show their review date and expiry; availability and prices can change before expiry. Historical vehicle records and illustrative examples are not live offers. Confirm specifications, fees, delivery, stock and final pricing with the seller. Calculators produce estimates, not guarantees of savings or resale profit.

Privacy during the pilot

The account service uses Supabase to process email addresses and authentication credentials, watchlist references and deal submissions. Passwords are submitted to the authentication service rather than stored by the website itself. The account page keeps its login session in memory. Browser-local watchlist references persist on your device until removed or browser storage is cleared. Authorised moderators may review submissions. Published offer records are separate from account identities. Do not include personal or confidential information in listing titles, URLs or descriptions.

The site is served through GitHub Pages and uses Supabase infrastructure. Those providers process requests under their own policies. The database project is hosted in Japan. See GitHub privacy information and Supabase privacy information.

Advertising and commercial relationships

Advertising placements are currently disabled. No advertising network is installed by BargainIQ. Future paid advertisements and sponsored offers will be labelled. Paid placements must remain separate from organic price comparison rankings. Affiliate relationships, when introduced, will be disclosed near relevant links.

Submissions

Only submit accurate information you are permitted to share. Submissions are subject to moderation; approval does not guarantee publication. We may withdraw outdated or inaccurate listings.

Pilot limitations

This is a development-stage notice, not final commercial terms or a complete production privacy policy. Public account registration should not be promoted until account recovery, contact and privacy request processes are established.
