Furniture that actually fits

Measure the space before comparing prices. For a TV unit, record maximum width, depth and height, allowing for door swing, skirting boards, ventilation and cable bends. Measure the route from the building entrance to the room, not just the wall. Ask for packaged dimensions and assembly requirements.

Compare delivered cost rather than the headline price. A lower-priced unit may cost more once freight or assembly is included. Check whether delivery means doorstep or room placement, whether returns require original packaging, and whether the finish is timber veneer, laminate or solid wood.

For a media unit, check the internal shelf opening rather than the outside dimensions. Leave space around consoles and amplifiers according to their manuals; don't place a heat-producing device in an enclosed compartment without adequate ventilation.

Shopping checklist: maximum W × D × H, internal shelf dimensions, delivery, assembly, finish, warranty and returns.

Choosing a television for your room

Start with viewing distance, room brightness and seating angles. Measure the available wall width and clearance around furniture, windows and air conditioning. Use the manufacturer's actual dimensions including stand if applicable, not the diagonal screen measurement.

OLED displays can deliver excellent black levels and contrast; LCD televisions with mini-LED backlights can be a strong option for bright rooms. Actual brightness, reflections, motion handling and viewing angles vary by model. Compare independent tests of the exact model rather than relying on panel labels alone.

For gaming, check the number of inputs that support the resolution and refresh rate your console or PC needs, variable refresh rate, and whether an audio return connection occupies one of those inputs. For sports, consider motion clarity and broadcast quality. Budget for mounting and sound separately.

Shopping checklist: exact model suffix, screen size, dimensions, inputs, warranty, delivered price and installation.

Compare appliances by total ownership cost

Check the appliance's dimensions, connections, clearance requirements and delivery route. For a fridge, account for door opening and ventilation; for a washing machine, check hoses, drainage, floor stability and access for servicing.

Compare the Australian energy label's annual consumption estimates for similar capacities and usage assumptions. Multiply annual kWh by your electricity tariff for an indicative running cost. Water consumption and detergent can matter for dishwashers and washing machines. The actual total depends on use and tariff.

Check installation, removal of the old unit, warranty and service access. A large discount is less attractive if delivery or installation is excluded. Keep a dated record of the exact model and price when comparing.

Shopping checklist: capacity, dimensions, annual kWh, water use where relevant, installation, removal and total delivered cost.

Gaming monitors: buy for your hardware

Choose a resolution and refresh rate that your graphics hardware can realistically drive in the games you play. A higher refresh rate is only useful when the system delivers suitable frame rates and the display handles motion well.

Compare independent measurements of response times, overshoot, input lag, contrast and variable refresh rate behaviour. A quoted response time alone is not enough. Check the supported resolution and refresh rate for each input, especially when connecting a console or laptop.

Measure desk depth and stand footprint, and check VESA mount compatibility if using an arm. Look at text clarity for work, brightness for the room and warranty conditions including pixel policies.

Shopping checklist: resolution, refresh rate, input specifications, measured motion performance, stand depth, warranty and delivered cost.

Compare new-car quotes fairly

Ask every dealer to quote the same model year, variant, powertrain, paint, options and delivery location. Specify whether the vehicle is new, demonstrator or used, and ask for odometer reading and build date where relevant.

Compare written drive-away totals for your registration location, with any conditional incentives clearly separated. If finance or a trade-in is involved, ask for a separate vehicle price, trade-in valuation and finance terms. A larger trade-in allowance is not necessarily a better overall transaction.

Confirm stock availability, delivery timing, included accessories, warranty commencement and deposit conditions in writing. A price on an old advertisement may not be available now. Avoid committing until you understand the contract and any finance obligations.

Shopping checklist: model year, variant, stock status, drive-away price, conditions, trade-in, finance costs and delivery estimate.

When a clearance deal is actually worth buying

Start with the product you need and a maximum total budget. Check the exact model, configuration and condition. A clearance listing may be discontinued, ex-display, refurbished or simply excess stock; those are not interchangeable.

Compare the current delivered price against other sellers of the identical product and condition. A crossed-out price is not proof of a genuine saving. Record observation dates, check delivery charges and avoid treating an unavailable listing as a usable benchmark.

Ask about missing accessories, packaging, cosmetic damage, warranty and return conditions. Australian consumer guarantees may still apply to discounted items bought from businesses; a change-of-mind policy is different. For official guidance see the ACCC consumer rights information.

Shopping checklist: condition, inclusions, observed price, date, shipping, warranty and returns.

How to choose a TV entertainment unit

Measure the wall and the route to it

Measure the available width at floor level and above skirting boards. Mark the depth on the floor with tape and walk past it with doors open. Check the packaged dimensions before arranging delivery or collecting furniture.

Check the equipment bay

List each console, receiver and soundbar with its manufacturer-specified ventilation requirements. Compare internal shelf dimensions, cable openings and access to power points. An external cabinet width tells you little about the usable shelf space.

Compare finishes and total cost

Ask whether a timber-look surface is solid timber, veneer or laminate. Consider cleaning requirements, assembly, freight and return costs. Record the exact finish and dimensions before comparing retailers.

Shopping checklist: TV unit dimensions, shelf openings, ventilation, cable access and delivered cost.

Choosing a coffee table for your living room

Start with movement

Mark the proposed table footprint on the floor. Walk between the couch, table and doorways. Test whether everyone can reach the table comfortably while seated. Rounded corners can reduce the impact of accidental bumps in tight rooms.

Match proportions and finishes

Compare height with the sofa seat and consider how you use the table. A lower table may suit a lounge; a taller surface can be easier to reach for drinks or games. Timber, stone-look and upholstered finishes have different care needs.

Check construction

Ask about the weight, stability, top material and assembly. Check whether a stone-look top is a coating or actual stone, and whether it needs protection from spills. Compare delivered cost, not just the advertised price.

Shopping checklist: Footprint, height, finish, cleaning instructions, stability and freight.

Buying a desk for a small home office

Measure working depth

Place a keyboard, mouse and monitor on your current surface and measure how much space they occupy. Account for the monitor stand or arm clamp, cable clearance and the distance you prefer to sit from the screen.

Plan equipment placement

Check drawer clearance for your knees and where a desktop computer will sit. If you use a monitor arm, confirm desktop thickness, material and load rating against the arm instructions. Avoid blocking ventilation or placing cables across walking routes.

Check delivery and setup

Compare the actual working surface rather than the overall unit width. Shelves and uprights can reduce usable space. Ask for packaging dimensions, assembly instructions and the desktop load rating before buying.

Shopping checklist: Usable width and depth, knee clearance, load rating, cable management and assembly.

Robot vacuum and mop buying checklist

Assess the floor plan

Identify thresholds, cables, rugs, low furniture and narrow gaps. Compare the manufacturer's clearance and climbing specifications with the actual rooms. A larger docking station may require more floor and wall clearance than the robot itself.

Compare cleaning features

For vacuuming, look at independent debris and hair pickup tests on your floor types. For mopping, compare how pads are washed, whether they lift over rugs, and how the robot handles edges. Claimed suction figures alone do not establish cleaning performance.

Budget for maintenance

Check replacement brush, filter and bag availability and prices. Look at dock cleaning requirements, mapping controls and privacy settings. For homes with pets, remove accidents and unsafe objects before running any robot.

Shopping checklist: Thresholds, dock dimensions, floor tests, consumables, privacy and warranty.

Choosing a home espresso machine

Match your daily routine

Decide whether you mainly drink espresso, long blacks or milk drinks. Consider the time you want to spend grinding, dosing, steaming and cleaning. A manual machine can offer control but also requires practice.

Compare grinder and workflow

A suitable grinder matters for espresso because small grind adjustments influence extraction. Compare adjustment range and consistency, the machine's heating workflow and whether making multiple drinks is convenient. Check that the included accessories suit your intended use.

Consider upkeep

Read the water, cleaning and descaling instructions for the exact model. Check availability of replacement seals, filters and service support. Budget separately for beans, cleaning supplies and a grinder if one is not included.

Shopping checklist: Drink workflow, grinder, heating, cleaning, accessories and servicing.

How to compare mattresses without relying on discounts

Identify your requirements

Consider preferred sleeping position, firmness preference, temperature comfort and whether you share a bed. Check the exact size against your frame, including any height restrictions for fitted sheets or bedding.

Understand the trial

Read the trial conditions before buying, including required trial periods, exclusions, collection arrangements and refund rules. A trial is not the same as a warranty. A comfort preference cannot reliably be predicted by the number of springs or foam layers alone.

Compare like with like

Check the exact model and size, base requirements, delivery and removal. Ask whether the advertised price depends on a bundle or code. If testing in person, try your usual sleeping positions rather than judging from a quick sit on the edge.

Shopping checklist: Size, comfort, base compatibility, trial terms, delivery and warranty.
