Furniture that actually fits

Measure the space before comparing prices. For a TV unit, record maximum width, depth and height, allowing for door swing, skirting boards, ventilation and cable bends. Measure the route from the building entrance to the room, not just the wall. Ask for packaged dimensions and assembly requirements.

Compare delivered cost rather than the headline price. A lower-priced unit may cost more once freight or assembly is included. Check whether delivery means doorstep or room placement, whether returns require original packaging, and whether the finish is timber veneer, laminate or solid wood.

For a media unit, check the internal shelf opening rather than the outside dimensions. Leave space around consoles and amplifiers according to their manuals; don't place a heat-producing device in an enclosed compartment without adequate ventilation.

Shopping checklist: maximum W × D × H, internal shelf dimensions, delivery, assembly, finish, warranty and returns.

Choosing a television for your room

Start with viewing distance, room brightness and seating angles. Measure the available wall width and clearance around furniture, windows and air conditioning. Use the manufacturer's actual dimensions including stand if applicable, not the diagonal screen measurement.

OLED displays can deliver excellent black levels and contrast; LCD televisions with mini-LED backlights can be a strong option for bright rooms. Actual brightness, reflections, motion handling and viewing angles vary by model. Compare independent tests of the exact model rather than relying on panel labels alone.

For gaming, check the number of inputs that support the resolution and refresh rate your console or PC needs, variable refresh rate, and whether an audio return connection occupies one of those inputs. For sports, consider motion clarity and broadcast quality. Budget for mounting and sound separately.

Shopping checklist: exact model suffix, screen size, dimensions, inputs, warranty, delivered price and installation.

Compare appliances by total ownership cost

Check the appliance's dimensions, connections, clearance requirements and delivery route. For a fridge, account for door opening and ventilation; for a washing machine, check hoses, drainage, floor stability and access for servicing.

Compare the Australian energy label's annual consumption estimates for similar capacities and usage assumptions. Multiply annual kWh by your electricity tariff for an indicative running cost. Water consumption and detergent can matter for dishwashers and washing machines. The actual total depends on use and tariff.

Check installation, removal of the old unit, warranty and service access. A large discount is less attractive if delivery or installation is excluded. Keep a dated record of the exact model and price when comparing.

Shopping checklist: capacity, dimensions, annual kWh, water use where relevant, installation, removal and total delivered cost.

Gaming monitors: buy for your hardware

Choose a resolution and refresh rate that your graphics hardware can realistically drive in the games you play. A higher refresh rate is only useful when the system delivers suitable frame rates and the display handles motion well.

Compare independent measurements of response times, overshoot, input lag, contrast and variable refresh rate behaviour. A quoted response time alone is not enough. Check the supported resolution and refresh rate for each input, especially when connecting a console or laptop.

Measure desk depth and stand footprint, and check VESA mount compatibility if using an arm. Look at text clarity for work, brightness for the room and warranty conditions including pixel policies.

Shopping checklist: resolution, refresh rate, input specifications, measured motion performance, stand depth, warranty and delivered cost.

Compare new-car quotes fairly

Ask every dealer to quote the same model year, variant, powertrain, paint, options and delivery location. Specify whether the vehicle is new, demonstrator or used, and ask for odometer reading and build date where relevant.

Compare written drive-away totals for your registration location, with any conditional incentives clearly separated. If finance or a trade-in is involved, ask for a separate vehicle price, trade-in valuation and finance terms. A larger trade-in allowance is not necessarily a better overall transaction.

Confirm stock availability, delivery timing, included accessories, warranty commencement and deposit conditions in writing. A price on an old advertisement may not be available now. Avoid committing until you understand the contract and any finance obligations.

Shopping checklist: model year, variant, stock status, drive-away price, conditions, trade-in, finance costs and delivery estimate.

When a clearance deal is actually worth buying

Start with the product you need and a maximum total budget. Check the exact model, configuration and condition. A clearance listing may be discontinued, ex-display, refurbished or simply excess stock; those are not interchangeable.

Compare the current delivered price against other sellers of the identical product and condition. A crossed-out price is not proof of a genuine saving. Record observation dates, check delivery charges and avoid treating an unavailable listing as a usable benchmark.

Ask about missing accessories, packaging, cosmetic damage, warranty and return conditions. Australian consumer guarantees may still apply to discounted items bought from businesses; a change-of-mind policy is different. For official guidance see the ACCC consumer rights information.

Shopping checklist: condition, inclusions, observed price, date, shipping, warranty and returns.

How to choose a TV entertainment unit

Measure the wall and the route to it

Measure the available width at floor level and above skirting boards. Mark the depth on the floor with tape and walk past it with doors open. Check the packaged dimensions before arranging delivery or collecting furniture.

Check the equipment bay

List each console, receiver and soundbar with its manufacturer-specified ventilation requirements. Compare internal shelf dimensions, cable openings and access to power points. An external cabinet width tells you little about the usable shelf space.

Compare finishes and total cost

Ask whether a timber-look surface is solid timber, veneer or laminate. Consider cleaning requirements, assembly, freight and return costs. Record the exact finish and dimensions before comparing retailers.

Shopping checklist: TV unit dimensions, shelf openings, ventilation, cable access and delivered cost.

Choosing a coffee table for your living room

Start with movement

Mark the proposed table footprint on the floor. Walk between the couch, table and doorways. Test whether everyone can reach the table comfortably while seated. Rounded corners can reduce the impact of accidental bumps in tight rooms.

Match proportions and finishes

Compare height with the sofa seat and consider how you use the table. A lower table may suit a lounge; a taller surface can be easier to reach for drinks or games. Timber, stone-look and upholstered finishes have different care needs.

Check construction

Ask about the weight, stability, top material and assembly. Check whether a stone-look top is a coating or actual stone, and whether it needs protection from spills. Compare delivered cost, not just the advertised price.

Shopping checklist: Footprint, height, finish, cleaning instructions, stability and freight.

Buying a desk for a small home office

Measure working depth

Place a keyboard, mouse and monitor on your current surface and measure how much space they occupy. Account for the monitor stand or arm clamp, cable clearance and the distance you prefer to sit from the screen.

Plan equipment placement

Check drawer clearance for your knees and where a desktop computer will sit. If you use a monitor arm, confirm desktop thickness, material and load rating against the arm instructions. Avoid blocking ventilation or placing cables across walking routes.

Check delivery and setup

Compare the actual working surface rather than the overall unit width. Shelves and uprights can reduce usable space. Ask for packaging dimensions, assembly instructions and the desktop load rating before buying.

Shopping checklist: Usable width and depth, knee clearance, load rating, cable management and assembly.

Robot vacuum and mop buying checklist

Assess the floor plan

Identify thresholds, cables, rugs, low furniture and narrow gaps. Compare the manufacturer's clearance and climbing specifications with the actual rooms. A larger docking station may require more floor and wall clearance than the robot itself.

Compare cleaning features

For vacuuming, look at independent debris and hair pickup tests on your floor types. For mopping, compare how pads are washed, whether they lift over rugs, and how the robot handles edges. Claimed suction figures alone do not establish cleaning performance.

Budget for maintenance

Check replacement brush, filter and bag availability and prices. Look at dock cleaning requirements, mapping controls and privacy settings. For homes with pets, remove accidents and unsafe objects before running any robot.

Shopping checklist: Thresholds, dock dimensions, floor tests, consumables, privacy and warranty.

Choosing a home espresso machine

Match your daily routine

Decide whether you mainly drink espresso, long blacks or milk drinks. Consider the time you want to spend grinding, dosing, steaming and cleaning. A manual machine can offer control but also requires practice.

Compare grinder and workflow

A suitable grinder matters for espresso because small grind adjustments influence extraction. Compare adjustment range and consistency, the machine's heating workflow and whether making multiple drinks is convenient. Check that the included accessories suit your intended use.

Consider upkeep

Read the water, cleaning and descaling instructions for the exact model. Check availability of replacement seals, filters and service support. Budget separately for beans, cleaning supplies and a grinder if one is not included.

Shopping checklist: Drink workflow, grinder, heating, cleaning, accessories and servicing.

How to compare mattresses without relying on discounts

Identify your requirements

Consider preferred sleeping position, firmness preference, temperature comfort and whether you share a bed. Check the exact size against your frame, including any height restrictions for fitted sheets or bedding.

Understand the trial

Read the trial conditions before buying, including required trial periods, exclusions, collection arrangements and refund rules. A trial is not the same as a warranty. A comfort preference cannot reliably be predicted by the number of springs or foam layers alone.

Compare like with like

Check the exact model and size, base requirements, delivery and removal. Ask whether the advertised price depends on a bundle or code. If testing in person, try your usual sleeping positions rather than judging from a quick sit on the edge.

Shopping checklist: Size, comfort, base compatibility, trial terms, delivery and warranty.

Choosing a sofa that fits your room

Measure the space

Tape the proposed footprint onto the floor, including chaise depth and recliner travel. Keep access to doors and windows clear. Measure the narrowest entrance and any stairs before ordering.

Compare comfort and materials

Try the seat depth and back support in your usual sitting position. Ask whether covers are removable, which cleaning methods are permitted and whether replacement cushions are available. Compare fabric samples in your own lighting.

Check delivery

Ask whether the sofa arrives in modules, whether legs detach and what delivery includes. Check return charges for bulky furniture and any exclusions for made-to-order colours.

Shopping checklist: Footprint, access, seat depth, fabric care, delivery and returns.

How to choose a soundbar for your TV

Check connections

Identify your television's audio output and the soundbar's supported inputs. HDMI ARC and eARC capabilities vary, so check both manuals for the audio formats you intend to use. Ensure the soundbar does not obstruct the TV sensor or screen.

Choose a layout

Measure the cabinet and identify power points for the bar, subwoofer and any rear speakers. Wireless speakers still commonly require power. Consider neighbours and shared walls before choosing a large subwoofer.

Compare listening performance

Use independent tests of dialogue clarity, bass extension and surround performance rather than advertised wattage alone. Check whether night mode and dialogue enhancement are available.

Shopping checklist: Dimensions, HDMI compatibility, speaker power, measured performance and warranty.

Planning a wall-mounted television

Check the mounting interface

Find the television's VESA pattern and weight without its stand. Choose a bracket rated for both. Confirm the required screw length and spacing with the TV and bracket instructions.

Plan wall and cable access

Identify the wall construction and suitable structural fixing points. Do not assume plasterboard alone can carry the load. Check for concealed services and use a qualified installer if the structure is uncertain. Fixed mains wiring must be handled by an appropriately licensed electrician.

Choose height before drilling

Mark the screen outline on the wall and check it from your usual seating position. Allow room for ventilation, connectors and soundbar placement. Consider how the TV will be removed for servicing.

Shopping checklist: VESA, weight, wall structure, viewing height, cable access and installation quote.

Choosing between two television sizes

Measure the actual screen

Diagonal size is not the screen width. For a 16:9 screen, width is approximately 0.872 times the diagonal and height 0.490 times the diagonal. A 65-inch panel is therefore about 144 cm wide before accounting for its frame.

Test the viewing experience

Mark both screen outlines using removable tape and sit at your usual viewing distance. Consider subtitles, sports and the quality of the content you watch. A bigger screen can make low-resolution material more noticeably imperfect.

Compare the trade-offs

A smaller model with better contrast, reflections or motion handling may suit the room better than a larger basic model. Compare the exact model, mounting costs and furniture clearance before choosing.

Shopping checklist: Screen width, seating distance, room light, exact model and mounting cost.

Setting up a TV for streaming and sport

Start with the connection

Check whether the television or streaming device supports your streaming apps. Test actual playback quality at busy times rather than assuming the internet plan speed reaches the device. Ethernet can help where wireless coverage is unreliable.

Compare picture settings

Begin with a manufacturer picture mode intended for your viewing environment. Adjust motion processing to preference: stronger smoothing can introduce artefacts. Keep separate settings for games if the television supports them.

Check subscription costs

Confirm whether the service includes the desired resolution, sports channels or simultaneous streams. Check whether the device supports the service's highest available format. Compare ongoing subscription costs as well as hardware prices.

Shopping checklist: App support, connection reliability, resolution, motion settings and subscription cost.

Comparing washing machines for your household

Match capacity to laundry

Estimate the size of a typical load rather than buying solely for the largest possible capacity. Check programme-specific load limits, especially for quick cycles. Bulky bedding may need a different drum size from everyday clothing.

Measure the installation

Measure the space with room for hoses, doors and servicing. Check whether the floor is suitable and whether water connections match the manual. For stacked appliances, confirm the manufacturer's approved stacking kit.

Compare operating costs

Compare annual energy and water figures for similar machines and test assumptions. Consider cycle duration, noise and access to local service. Ask about installation and old appliance removal.

Shopping checklist: Capacity, programme limits, dimensions, energy, water and installation.

Choosing an air fryer or multicooker

Choose the cooking task

An air fryer is suited to circulating hot air around food, while a pressure multicooker can suit stews, beans and other moist dishes. List the meals you actually cook before choosing an appliance.

Check useful capacity

Compare basket shape and usable cooking area rather than litres alone. A large basket may be more useful for a single layer of food. For pressure cookers, follow the manufacturer's maximum fill lines and operating instructions.

Plan storage and cleaning

Measure bench space and cupboard height, allowing for heat clearance and lid opening. Check dishwasher-safe parts, replacement accessories and cleaning instructions. Avoid treating preset counts as evidence of cooking quality.

Shopping checklist: Cooking tasks, usable capacity, clearances, cleaning and accessories.

Choosing a laptop for everyday work

Identify workload

List the applications you need and their recommended hardware specifications. Browser-based office work differs from video editing, CAD or gaming. Check operating system compatibility before comparing hardware.

Compare practical performance

Look at independent battery and thermal tests for the exact configuration. Memory capacity, storage and screen quality can be more important to everyday comfort than a processor badge. Check whether memory and storage can be upgraded.

Check portability

Consider charger weight, port selection, keyboard layout and external monitor support. Compare warranty service and repair options. Confirm the advertised configuration matches the retailer listing.

Shopping checklist: Applications, RAM, storage, display, battery tests, ports and warranty.

Comparing gaming desktop PCs

Start with the games

Choose the resolution and frame-rate target for the games you play. Compare benchmarks using similar settings and hardware. A balanced system matters more than a single impressive component.

Read the full parts list

Check the exact graphics card, processor, memory configuration, storage, power supply and cooling. Ask for specific part models where a listing says only a generic capacity or wattage. Check component compatibility and upgrade space.

Compare the complete cost

Include operating system, peripherals and delivery if not supplied. Check warranty responsibility for the assembled system and whether opening it affects any support conditions. Avoid comparing different graphics card variants as identical.

Shopping checklist: Exact parts, game benchmarks, power supply, cooling, upgrades and total cost.

Choosing a home Wi-Fi router

Identify the internet connection

Check the connection type and whether your provider requires a modem, specific authentication or voice service support. A router alone does not replace every type of modem. Confirm compatibility with your provider.

Assess coverage

Identify rooms with poor reception and likely router placement. Walls, neighbouring networks and device capabilities affect performance. Compare a single router with wired access points or mesh if coverage is the real problem.

Compare useful features

Check wired port speeds, security updates, guest networks and parental controls if needed. Faster advertised wireless rates are theoretical and shared across devices. Avoid paying for a feature that your devices cannot use.

Shopping checklist: Connection compatibility, coverage, ports, update policy and total hardware cost.

Comparing used-car listings fairly

Compare equivalent vehicles

Check model year, variant, transmission, kilometres and condition. Review service records and ask about accident repairs or outstanding faults. Do not assume two vehicles with the same model name have the same equipment.

Verify history and condition

Use the official Australian PPSR service to check relevant vehicle history and security interests using the correct VIN. Consider an independent inspection before purchase. A history check does not replace a mechanical inspection.

Compare purchase costs

Ask about registration, transfer costs, dealer charges and immediate maintenance needs. Check whether the price is drive-away or excludes government charges. Record written offers and their conditions.

Shopping checklist: Variant, kilometres, history, inspection, registration and total cost.

Should you buy a demonstrator vehicle?

Understand its use

Ask when the car was first registered, its current odometer reading and whether it has been used for test drives or staff transport. Inspect tyres, paint, interior and accessories rather than assuming it is equivalent to untouched stock.

Compare warranty dates

Ask for the remaining manufacturer warranty and servicing schedule in writing. Check whether any new-car promotion applies to the demonstrator and whether registration or delivery conditions differ.

Compare actual alternatives

Obtain a written quote for an equivalent new vehicle and compare drive-away totals, kilometres, condition and delivery timing. A demonstrator is only a better deal if the overall trade-off suits you.

Shopping checklist: Registration date, kilometres, condition, warranty, new-car alternative and price.

How to compare car finance quotes

Separate the vehicle deal

Request the cash drive-away price independently from finance and trade-in negotiations. Compare the same loan amount, term and deposit across quotes. Ask whether a promotional price depends on using a particular lender.

Compare the whole repayment

Review interest rate, comparison rate where applicable, establishment fees, ongoing fees and any balloon payment. A lower regular payment may come from a longer term or a large final payment. Ask for the total amount payable.

Check flexibility

Read early repayment, discharge and missed-payment conditions. Make sure repayments fit your budget and consider independent financial guidance if needed. Do not sign based on a weekly payment alone.

Shopping checklist: Cash price, loan amount, term, fees, balloon and total payable.

Estimating the cost of owning a car

Calculate fuel or electricity

Estimate annual kilometres and realistic consumption for your driving. For petrol, multiply kilometres by litres per 100 km divided by 100, then by fuel price. For EVs, use kWh per 100 km and your expected charging tariffs, allowing for losses where relevant.

Include fixed expenses

Collect actual insurance quotes, registration costs and scheduled servicing estimates for the exact vehicle. Allow for tyres and unexpected maintenance. Different drivers and locations can produce very different insurance costs.

Compare resale uncertainty

Depreciation can be a major expense but future resale values are uncertain. Model several resale scenarios rather than treating an advertised prediction as guaranteed. Include finance costs if applicable.

Shopping checklist: Annual distance, energy, insurance, registration, servicing, tyres and depreciation scenarios.

How to compare delivery costs when shopping online

Use your postcode

Enter the delivery postcode before comparing total prices. Freight can vary by location, item size and seller. Check whether free delivery thresholds apply to the item and whether remote area or bulky freight exclusions exist.

Understand delivery service

Ask whether delivery is kerbside, doorstep or room placement. Confirm access requirements, booking windows and charges for stairs. For assembled goods, check whether packaging removal or installation is included.

Check return freight

Read the return policy before ordering, including bulky item costs and change-of-mind exclusions. Keep screenshots of the order and quoted delivery terms. Consumer guarantee remedies are separate from voluntary change-of-mind policies.

Shopping checklist: Postcode, freight, delivery service, access, installation and return terms.

How to interpret a product price history

Check the exact identity

Compare the same model, size, colour, pack and condition. A cheaper variant or refurbished item should not be treated as a price drop for a different configuration. Keep retailer identities separate when tracking individual offers.

Understand the observation window

A recorded low is only the lowest price in the available observations, not necessarily the lowest price ever. Gaps in observations can hide short promotions. Check dates and whether prices include delivery.

Decide against your budget

Set a target delivered price based on your needs and comparable current offers. Do not buy solely because a percentage discount looks large. Confirm the current price, availability and any coupon conditions at checkout.

Shopping checklist: Exact product, retailer, observation dates, delivery, target price and current availability.

Buying refurbished and ex-display products

Clarify condition

Ask what the condition label actually means. Refurbished, used, open-box and ex-display can describe different histories. Confirm whether functional testing has occurred and whether cosmetic damage is documented.

Check inclusions

Ask about original accessories, chargers, packaging and replacement parts. For battery-powered products, ask whether battery health is measured and what standard is guaranteed. Check whether the retailer or manufacturer provides any warranty.

Compare with new

Compare the total cost with a new equivalent, accounting for missing accessories and possible replacement parts. Read return terms and consider consumer guarantees where buying from a business.

Shopping checklist: Condition, testing, accessories, battery health where relevant, warranty and delivered cost.

How to write a useful shopping request

Start with non-negotiables

Write down the intended use, maximum budget and the physical constraints that cannot change. Separate must-have specifications from preferences. For furniture, distinguish maximum outside dimensions from minimum internal space.

Specify the price basis

Say whether your budget includes delivery, installation or accessories. Include your general delivery region when checking retailer costs, but avoid sharing your full address in public requests. Specify acceptable conditions such as new or refurbished.

Review the results

Check that each suggested product meets every required specification. Missing data should be treated as unknown, not as a match. Verify the exact retailer listing and current price before buying.

Shopping checklist: Product type, budget basis, measurements, condition, must-haves and flexible preferences.
