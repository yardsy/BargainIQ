# Search and competitor research — 26 November 2026

This supports the permanent master roadmap; it is not a separate roadmap. Search phrases below are hypotheses, not measured volume, difficulty or guaranteed rankings.

## Competitor evidence
- BuyWisely: broad price tracking and price history. https://buywisely.com.au/au/about
- Bonfind: delivered-price comparison, including furniture. https://bonfind.com/au
- Half Off Home: homewares and furniture URL-based price history. https://www.halfoffhome.com.au/price-check
- PriceHound: Australian price comparisons and historical observations. https://pricehound.com.au/
- Dealwatch: recorded prices and discount reference labels. https://dealwatch.com.au/about.html
- CHOICE independent tracker comparison: https://www.choice.com.au/shopping/online-shopping/buying-online/articles/price-trackers

Do not claim these services lack features without testing. Generic price tracking and delivery-inclusive pricing are not unique. Test a requirements-first workflow combining external and internal dimensions, compatibility, condition, postcode-specific delivery and maximum total budget. Unknown specifications must not count as matches. Authorised inventory coverage remains the primary constraint.
