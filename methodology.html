How we compare products

Our purpose is to help Australian shoppers find products that meet their requirements, not simply products with the largest advertised discounts. BargainIQ is currently a beta with limited inventory coverage.

Editorial guidance

Our buying guides explain practical selection criteria and include shopping checklists. They are general editorial guidance, not hands-on product tests. We do not claim to have personally tested products unless that is documented. Illustrations are explanatory, not retailer product photographs. Product specifications should be checked against the manufacturer's current documentation.

Like-for-like comparison

We compare products using reliable model identifiers and matching condition, pack and variant information. Where an identity or required specification is missing, we do not treat it as confirmed. Used, refurbished and new products are not interchangeable. Delivery-inclusive comparisons require a known delivery charge; local delivery may differ.

Prices and availability

Recorded prices are observations, not guarantees. Historical lows refer only to the recorded observation window. An advertised reference price is not necessarily a genuine previous selling price. We distinguish historical vehicle listings from recently verified offers. Always confirm current availability, the exact model, delivery and final price with the retailer.

Sources and commercial relationships

Our catalogue is limited to data we are permitted to display or offers checked and published through our moderation process. We do not fabricate listings, ratings, stock levels or traffic. Affiliate links and paid placements are not active at present. If introduced, commissions and sponsorship will be disclosed and paid placements kept separate from organic results.

Limitations and corrections

We do not cover every retailer or guarantee the cheapest price. Check the underlying source and report inaccurate listings through the account submission process where available. For consumer rights, see ACCC consumer guidance. For energy labels, see Energy Rating.
